1. Procurement – Purchase Orders
10 prompts
These prompts cover purchase orders, PO lines, schedules, distributions, and buyers.
#	Prompt
1	Show me all purchase orders created in the last 30 days. Include PO number, supplier name, buyer, creation date, total amount, and status.
2	Get the complete details of purchase order {PO_NUMBER}, including supplier, business unit, buyer, currency, total amount, and approval status.
3	Show all lines for purchase order {PO_NUMBER}. Include item description, quantity, unit price, UOM, and line amount.
4	Show the shipment schedules for purchase order {PO_NUMBER}. Include shipment number, ordered quantity, received quantity, promised date, and shipment status.
5	Show the distribution details for purchase order {PO_NUMBER}. Include distribution number, charge account, quantity, amount, and deliver-to location.
6	Find all purchase orders for supplier {SUPPLIER_NAME}. Show PO number, order date, total amount, and status.
7	Show all purchase orders assigned to buyer {BUYER_NAME}. Include PO number, supplier, amount, and approval status.
8	Find purchase orders that are pending approval. Show PO number, supplier, buyer, amount, and submission date.
9	Show purchase orders with pending or overdue shipments. Include PO number, supplier, shipment number, promised delivery date, and outstanding quantity.
10	Get the total purchase order amount by supplier for the current month. Display supplier name, number of purchase orders, and total amount.

10 prompts

#	Prompt

3	Show all invoice lines for invoice {INVOICE_NUMBER}. Include line number, description, quantity, unit price, amount, and line type.
4	Find all unpaid invoices for supplier {SUPPLIER_NAME}. Show invoice number, invoice date, due date, amount, and remaining balance.
5	Show all invoices currently on hold. Include invoice number, supplier, invoice amount, hold reason, and hold status.
6	Find all overdue supplier invoices. Show invoice number, supplier, due date, outstanding amount, and number of overdue days.
7	Show the payment details for invoice {INVOICE_NUMBER}. Include payment number, payment date, payment method, payment amount, and payment status.
8	Check whether invoice {INVOICE_NUMBER} has completed validation and accounting. Show validation status, accounting status, and any errors.
9	Find duplicate invoices for supplier {SUPPLIER_NAME}. Compare invoice numbers, invoice dates, and invoice amounts.
10	Show the total outstanding invoice amount grouped by supplier for the current month. Include supplier name, invoice count, and outstanding amount.
