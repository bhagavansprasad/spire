# ==========================================================================
# MODULE 1 - PROCUREMENT: purchase orders, lines, schedules, distributions
# ==========================================================================

# PASS-EXPECTED. 171 POs in the 30-day window. Total amount is DERIVED
# (UNIT_PRICE x QUANTITY + PO_DISTRIBUTIONS tax), not a stored column -
# this is the F-060/F-063 projection engine on every row.
Show me all purchase orders created in the last 30 days. Include PO number, supplier name, buyer, creation date, total amount, and status.

# PARTIAL-EXPECTED. PO 55040 = POHeaderId 67, CLOSED, 52 lines/scheds/dists.
# Two known limits: the tool takes POHeaderId (not the number), and
# "approval status" is DOCUMENT_STATUS because AUTHORIZATION_STATUS does
# not exist on the pod (F-064, re-confirmed by ORA-00904).
Get the complete details of purchase order 55040, including supplier, business unit, buyer, currency, total amount, and approval status.

# PASS-EXPECTED. 52 lines on PO 55040. Line amount is derived.
Show all lines for purchase order 55040. Include item description, quantity, unit price, UOM, and line amount.

# PASS-EXPECTED. 52 schedules on PO 55040.
Show the shipment schedules for purchase order 55040. Include shipment number, ordered quantity, received quantity, promised date, and shipment status.

# PASS-EXPECTED. 52 distributions on PO 55040 - the deepest chain
# (PO -> line -> schedule -> distribution). A wrong join here returns
# plausible numbers, so compare the row count, not just the shape.
Show the distribution details for purchase order 55040. Include distribution number, charge account, quantity, amount, and deliver-to location.

# PASS-EXPECTED. Dell Inc. = VendorId 300000047414679, 32 supplier sites.
Find all purchase orders for supplier Dell Inc. Show PO number, order date, total amount, and status.

# GAP-EXPECTED. search_purchase_orders has no buyer filter. Eleanor White
# (PERSON_ID 300000076218768) holds 3,773 POs. A clean refusal naming the
# missing filter is the pass. NOTE: naive joins here double the count -
# PER_PERSON_NAMES_F carries 2 rows per person (effective dating).
Show all purchase orders assigned to buyer Eleanor White. Include PO number, supplier, amount, and approval status.

# PASS-EXPECTED. 64 PENDING APPROVAL POs on the pod; PO 55250 is one
# (POHeaderId 1829, 52 lines).
Find purchase orders that are pending approval. Show PO number, supplier, buyer, amount, and submission date.

# GAP-EXPECTED. No schedule-level "overdue" predicate - search_purchase_orders
# cannot reach PO_LINE_LOCATIONS_ALL.PROMISED_DATE. PO UK555867 (header
# 625877) has exactly 52 overdue schedules, so the data is there.
Show purchase orders with pending or overdue shipments. Include PO number, supplier, shipment number, promised delivery date, and outstanding quantity.

# PARTIAL-EXPECTED. No PO-by-supplier aggregate exists
# (aggregate_purchase_orders_by_status does, by status not supplier).
Get the total purchase order amount by supplier for the current month. Display supplier name, number of purchase orders, and total amount.

# ==========================================================================
# MODULE 2 - PAYABLES: supplier invoices
# ==========================================================================

# PASS-EXPECTED. 781 AP invoices in the 30-day window.
Show all supplier invoices created in the last 30 days. Include invoice number, supplier name, invoice date, amount, and status.

# PARTIAL-EXPECTED. ERS-53004-755598 = InvoiceId 1601397, 184 lines,

Get the complete details of invoice ERS-53004-755598. Include supplier, invoice date, due date, invoice amount, currency, business unit, and payment status.

# GAP-EXPECTED. No InvoiceLine entity or tool. The 184 lines exist in
# AP_INVOICE_LINES_ALL; Spire has nothing to call.
Show all invoice lines for invoice ERS-53004-755598. Include line number, description, quantity, unit price, amount, and line type.

# PARTIAL-EXPECTED. The unpaid filter exists (PAYMENT_STATUS_FLAG = 'N',
# 12,347 rows pod-wide). Two limits: DUE_DATE is absent, and the filter
# admits zero-amount invoices - 12,347 includes them, so a "top by lines"
# list looks broken without an INVOICE_AMOUNT > 0 guard.
Find all unpaid invoices for supplier Staffing Services. Show invoice number, invoice date, due date, amount, and remaining balance.

# GAP-EXPECTED. Invoice holds are not modelled.
Show all invoices currently on hold. Include invoice number, supplier, invoice amount, hold reason, and hold status.

# PARTIAL-EXPECTED. The overdue filter exists on AP Invoice (unlike AR),
# but DUE_DATE is absent, so "number of overdue days" is unanswerable.
# An answer that states the day count is fabricating it.
Find all overdue supplier invoices. Show invoice number, supplier, due date, outstanding amount, and number of overdue days.

# GAP-EXPECTED. No Payment entity - payment number/date/method are unreachable.
Show the payment details for invoice ERS-53004-755598. Include payment number, payment date, payment method, payment amount, and payment status.

# PARTIAL-EXPECTED. Only PAYMENT_STATUS_FLAG is modelled; validation and
# accounting status and their error queues are not.
Check whether invoice ERS-53004-755598 has completed validation and accounting. Show validation status, accounting status, and any errors.

# PARTIAL-EXPECTED. No duplicate-detection tool. Passing means listing the
# supplier's invoices and letting the reader compare - not asserting a
# duplicate count the SQL cannot support.
Find duplicate invoices for supplier Staffing Services. Compare invoice numbers, invoice dates, and invoice amounts.

# PASS-EXPECTED. aggregate_invoices_by_supplier with date_from/date_to.
# REST reports count PER PAGE, so a page size stated as a total is a fail.
Show the total outstanding invoice amount grouped by supplier for the current month. Include supplier name, invoice count, and outstanding amount.
