Choose from exactly these four modules: payables, procurement, receivables, supplier. Pick the module whose entities — or their listed synonyms — the question names. For a question that clearly concerns one area, return that single module.

A caution on one shared word. A question about the status or progress of ONE named purchase order — whether PO N has been received, invoiced, or paid; its lifecycle stage; what is blocking it; a summary of it — is a PROCUREMENT question, even though it says "invoiced" or "paid." Those are stages of that purchase order's lifecycle, reported from the order itself; they do not make it a payables question. Do NOT add payables for such a question.
